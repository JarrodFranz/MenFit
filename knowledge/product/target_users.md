# Target Users

*Last updated: 2026-08-25 (bootstrap). Provisional — refine with real product research.*

## Primary

**Women who run or want to run**, roughly 18–45, across ability levels:

- **Returning/beginner runners** — want gentle progression, streaks/motivation that don't punish them for physiology (period, low-energy phases), safety reassurance on solo runs.
- **Habitual recreational runners** — track runs, routes, PRs; want cycle context on why some weeks feel harder; log strength work and food alongside.
- **Performance-oriented amateurs** — training load, cycle-phase-aware planning, analytics, nutrition periodisation.

## Secondary

- Women focused on **strength training and nutrition** who run occasionally.
- **Pregnant/postpartum** users (future; requires careful, evidence-based product work).
- **Trusted contacts** — friends/family (any gender) who receive live-tracking links; they may use a lightweight web view without an account.

## Key user needs to keep in view

- Safety when exercising alone (live tracking, visible-to-whom controls).
- Motivation mechanics that respect the menstrual cycle instead of breaking streaks.
- Absolute confidence that cycle/health data is private and under the user's control.
- Low-friction logging (runs auto-tracked; food logging fast).
- Works on the devices they have — phone first, but web/desktop for review and planning.
