# Target Users

*Last updated: 2026-09-07 at the fork from FemFit (ADR 017). Provisional — refine with real product research.*

## Primary

**Men who lift and/or run**, roughly 18–50, across ability levels:

- **Returning/beginner trainees** — want gentle progression, streaks/motivation that don't punish them for a missed session, illness or injury.
- **Habitual recreational lifters/runners** — track workouts and runs, routes, PRs; log nutrition alongside; want honest recovery tracking rather than a broken streak.
- **Performance-oriented amateurs** — training load, mesocycle planning, analytics, nutrition periodisation (bulk/cut/maintain).

## Secondary

- Men focused on **strength training and nutrition** who run occasionally.
- **Trusted contacts** — friends/family (any gender) who receive live-tracking links; they may use a lightweight web view without an account.

## Key user needs to keep in view

- Safety when training alone (live tracking, visible-to-whom controls).
- Motivation mechanics that respect illness, injury and life instead of breaking streaks.
- Absolute confidence that health, flare and location data is private and under the user's control.
- Low-friction logging (runs auto-tracked; food logging fast).
- Works on the devices they have — phone first, but web/desktop for review and planning.
